description: Why agents decide quietly or ask numbered questions, never record assumptions: in four days 13 of 1,085 logged assumptions were challenged while one in ten questions changed course
---

From 2026-10-14 to 10-18 agents logged every call made without asking as an
`## Assumptions` entry for the person to challenge. Over four days 13 of
1,085 were challenged, while about one in ten questions put to the person
changed course, so assumptions were dropped: an agent makes a call the
person need not settle and asks only when a wrong guess is costly, hard to
undo, or against what the person said. Stats counts only questions, and
older outcome lines keep assumption counts it skips.

Questions are numbered open questions in `## Blocked` and in the reply, which
the page shows as a card with the agent's pick chosen; Claude's
AskUserQuestion tool is off in sessions because its form answers never
reached the plan, and `splates escalate` was retired, with waiting, review
and retry read from replies so "waiting on you" survives a reload.
